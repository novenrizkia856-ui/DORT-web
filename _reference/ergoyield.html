Leverage

Real leverage on memes

A small premium controls a large notional. When the coin runs, the call pays a multiple of what you put in — the same convexity a perp gives you, without the borrow.

Safety

No liquidations

You are not borrowing, so there is nothing to liquidate. A wick down can't close your position, and a quiet week can't bleed you out through funding.

Asymmetry

Fixed loss, unlimited profit

Your downside is the premium, priced before you click buy. Your upside keeps going for as long as the coin does. That asymmetry is the whole trade.
